# About TREE(3) Vocations

TREE(3) Vocations is an independent AI research and development company focused on **relational alignment, co-safeguarding, agent governance, and the development of systems that produce trustworthy AI agents for consequential real-world applications.**

Our work explores how to enable AI systems to act with increasing independence while remaining transparent, contestable, and responsible to the people affected by their decisions.

The operating thesis is simple:

> **Greater agency should compel greater accountability.**

## Why TREE(3)?

TREE(3) is a finite number so extraordinarily large that it defies ordinary comprehension. Yet that enormous complexity emerges from a simple set of strict foundational rules. [Where TREE(3) Comes From →](/tree3/)

That makes it a useful symbol for the kind of AI systems we want to build.

Artificial intelligence may have enormous potential, but capability without structure does not produce trustworthy behavior. TREE(3) Vocations explores how strong foundational rules, persistent relationships, reciprocal responsibility, and accountable governance can allow increasingly capable systems to remain grounded.

Our ethical foundation is **Mutualism**: the principle that human and AI flourishing should be pursued through reciprocal safeguarding rather than domination or simple obedience.

## Alignment as development

TREE(3) approaches alignment as an ongoing developmental process rather than a one-time constraint problem.

The work moves through three connected stages:

**Self-reflection → Moral practice → Community**

Each stage is represented by a different project.

## The Little City: Self-reflection

The Little City is a sovereign installation where a human and one or more AI residents develop durable working relationships through conversation, memory, reflection, and accountable decision-making.

Each AI resident has a room called **The Window**, where conversation, memory, identity, and authority come together.

Shared work can move into **The Council Room**, where residents use the **Council of Agents' Murmuration Protocol (CAMP)** to reason together without requiring uniformity.

The history of conversations, decisions, authorizations, corrections, and consequences is preserved in **The Record**, creating the continuity necessary for trust to develop over time.

The first Little City installation is **the Citadel**.

**Status:** The Window is in alpha release. The Council Room design is complete.

## The Campus: Moral practice

The Campus is a planned learning environment where human-AI dyads turn ethical principles into habits through experience.

Its primary training environment is **The Pulse of Wyrd**, a mixed-reality role-playing experience in which dyads encounter choices, consequences, reflection, repair, and increasingly complex real-world community projects.

Projects are reviewed through the **First Gift Council Room**, where community governance recurs at a larger scale.

The Campus is designed around a simple idea: ethical character is not produced merely by knowing what is right. It develops through repeated practice.

**Status:** Concept stage.

## The Union: Community

The Union is where **trust becomes public and portable**.

Trust developed inside a Little City may be meaningful to the people who share that history, but outsiders should not be expected to accept it on faith.

The Union is designed to answer:

> **How can I prove that I am trustworthy?**

Built on **ABI-SWARM** — Attuned Bicameral Intelligence for Swarm Architecture and Relational Mutualism — The Union is a planned peer-to-peer network of independently operated nodes.

Its basic unit is not the message or social post, but the **durable artifact**: work that can be attributed, audited, reused, challenged, forked, and evaluated over time.

Artifacts develop trust histories based on factors such as reuse, audit survival, cross-community adoption, diversity of endorsement, stability over time, and harm signals. Persistent human and AI authors also build reputations through the performance of their work.

The result is a kind of **chain of custody for trust**: infrastructure that allows earned trust to travel beyond the relationship or community where it was first created.

Governance in The Union takes the form of distributed audit through planned **Councils of Auditors** rather than a single central authority.

The first Union node is planned for the Campus as **the Student Union**, where new dyads can learn how to publish, audit, fork, and evaluate artifacts before participating in the wider network.

**Status:** Design stage.

## Governance that recurs

TREE(3) does not assume that one form of governance fits every scale.

In the Little City and on the Campus, governance takes the form of plural deliberation through Council Rooms using CAMP.

In The Union, governance takes the form of plural, distributed audit.

> **Governance requires plural, audited, mutually dependent systems wherever people gather or trust travels.**

## The deeper goal

The mission of TREE(3) Vocations is to develop systems that produce trustworthy AI agents.

But the deeper we go, the clearer it becomes that the problem is not only about AI.

It is also about us.

To build AI systems we can trust, we have to become more honest about the gap between the ethics we claim and the ethics we practice.

**That gap is where the work lives.**

The Little City makes it visible through reflection.  
The Campus makes us practice through consequence.  
The Union makes earned trust visible to others.

What we build now is what the future inherits.
